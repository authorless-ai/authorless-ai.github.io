  - q: "What is structured data and why does it matter for content?"
    a:
      - "Structured data tells search engines what your content means, not just what it says. Bazam's FAQ pages include automatic JSON-LD structured data — making your Q&As eligible for Google rich results and AI citation. More visibility in search results means more clicks."

  - q: "How do I add FAQ schema to my website?"
    a:
      - "Use FAQPage JSON-LD markup. Bazam generates this automatically for every FAQ page — each question and answer is marked up with schema.org structured data. Search engines and AI systems can read and quote your content directly."

  - q: "How does structured data help with AI search?"
    a:
      - "AI search engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity) prefer structured, machine-readable content. Bazam's FAQ pages include JSON-LD schema that makes your content directly quotable by AI systems. Your brand gets attributed when AI answers questions."

  - q: "How do I optimise my blog for mobile?"
    a:
      - "Mobile-first design, fast loading times, and readable formatting. Bazam's blog wizard creates content with short paragraphs, clear headings, and scannable structure — formats that work well on mobile screens."

  - q: "How does page speed affect content ranking?"
    a:
      - "Slow pages rank lower and lose visitors. Optimise images, minimise code bloat, and choose fast hosting. Bazam publishes clean, structured content to your WordPress — the content side is optimised. Ensure your hosting and theme don't add unnecessary weight."

  - q: "How do I create a site structure that helps SEO?"
    a:
      - "Organise content into logical categories with clear navigation. Create topic clusters — pillar pages linking to supporting articles. Bazam's content across categories naturally creates this structure as you publish FAQ clusters, blog post series, and topic-specific pages."

  - q: "What are internal links and why do they matter?"
    a:
      - "Links between your own pages that pass authority and help users navigate. As your content library grows with Bazam, internal linking opportunities multiply. Link FAQ pages to related blog posts, blog posts to feature pages, and create a web of connected content."

  - q: "How do I create content for Google's featured snippets?"
    a:
      - "Answer specific questions directly in the first sentence, then elaborate. Bazam's FAQ format is ideal — each Q&A starts with a direct, quotable answer followed by supporting detail. This format is what Google looks for in featured snippet candidates."

  - q: "How do I make my content AI-friendly?"
    a:
      - "Structure it clearly, include structured data markup, and make answers directly quotable. Bazam's FAQ pages do all three — clear Q&A format, JSON-LD schema, and first-sentence answers designed to be cited. AI optimisation is built into the content format."

  - q: "How do I fix duplicate content issues?"
    a:
      - "Ensure every page has unique content. Use canonical tags for similar pages. Bazam creates unique content for each page — the AI doesn't duplicate between FAQ pages or blog posts. Each piece is original, targeting a specific topic."
