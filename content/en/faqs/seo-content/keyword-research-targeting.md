description: "How to find keywords, target search intent, and create content that matches what your audience is searching for."
faq:
  - q: "How do I find content topics based on search intent?"
    a:
      - "Bazam's agent suggests topics based on your audience's interests and needs. It identifies the questions your potential customers are asking and creates content that directly addresses them. Each piece targets real search intent, not vanity keywords."

  - q: "What is search intent and why does it matter?"
    a:
      - "Search intent is what someone actually wants when they type a query. Informational (learning something), navigational (finding something), commercial (comparing options), or transactional (buying something). Bazam creates content that matches the right intent for each topic."

  - q: "How do I create content for long-tail keywords?"
    a:
      - "Target specific questions and scenarios. Instead of 'marketing' (impossible to rank), target 'how do I create social media content for a small bakery.' Bazam's agent creates specific, detailed answers to niche questions — exactly what long-tail SEO requires."

  - q: "How do I create content that answers questions?"
    a:
      - "Use an FAQ format and direct-answer structure. Bazam's FAQ output is designed exactly for this — each answer starts with a clear, direct response that search engines can quote directly. This format is ideal for featured snippets and AI Overview responses."

  - q: "What kinds of content rank best on Google?"
    a:
      - "Comprehensive, well-structured answers to specific questions. Google rewards content that thoroughly covers a topic with headings, relevant detail, and genuine expertise. Bazam creates this kind of content naturally from your knowledge base."

  - q: "How do I avoid keyword stuffing with AI content?"
    a:
      - "Use Bazam. The agent creates natural, readable content that organically incorporates relevant terms. Because it writes for people first — not algorithms — the content never feels forced or keyword-stuffed."

  - q: "How do I create content clusters for SEO?"
    a:
      - "Identify a broad topic (pillar) and create Bazam content covering every subtopic around it. Each cluster article focuses on a specific question or angle. Link them together. This builds topical authority that search engines reward with higher rankings."

  - q: "Should I update old blog posts for SEO?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Refreshing older content with new data, expanded coverage, and updated information signals to Google that your content is current. Ask Bazam's agent to update and expand existing pieces — keeping the URL while improving the content."

  - q: "How do I optimise existing content for better rankings?"
    a:
      - "Expand thin content, add more detail, improve structure, and ensure it comprehensively answers the target query. Bazam's agent can enhance existing content — adding depth, refining structure, and incorporating knowledge base context."

  - q: "What is the best SEO content format?"
    a:
      - "Comprehensive guides, FAQ pages, how-to articles, and comparison pieces. Each format targets different search intents. Bazam creates all of them — and its FAQ format is specifically designed to earn featured snippets and AI Overview citations."
