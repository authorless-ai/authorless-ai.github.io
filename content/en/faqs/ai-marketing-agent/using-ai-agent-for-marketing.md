faq:
  - q: "How do I use AI for marketing without learning prompt engineering?"
    a:
      - "Talk to Bazam's agent like you'd talk to a colleague. 'Write me a blog post about our new product', 'How did last week's content perform?', 'Create 10 social posts for our summer campaign.' No special syntax, no prompt templates, no jargon. Natural conversation is the interface."

  - q: "Can AI pull marketing analytics and create reports?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Bazam's AI agent tracks engagement, reach, clicks, impressions, and follower growth across all connected channels. Ask it 'how did we perform this month?' and it gives you a plain-English summary with the numbers that matter. No more logging into five dashboards."

  - q: "Can AI manage marketing campaigns?"
    a:
      - "Bazam's agent creates content for campaigns, schedules it across platforms, and tracks performance — all from a single conversation. You define campaign goals, target audience, and themes; the agent handles production, distribution, and reporting."

  - q: "How do I get AI to create content in my brand voice?"
    a:
      - "Set up your brand voice in Bazam once — tone, formality, style, custom guidelines, and example content. The agent applies these to everything it creates. Your voice persists across every conversation and every team member who uses the platform."

  - q: "Can an AI agent handle multiple marketing channels?"
    a:
      - "Bazam's agent publishes to WordPress, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, and TikTok from a single conversation. It creates platform-specific content, schedules each piece for optimal timing, and tracks performance across all channels in one dashboard."

  - q: "How do I brainstorm marketing ideas with AI?"
    a:
      - "Ask the agent. 'Give me 10 campaign ideas for our B2B audience' or 'What blog topics should we cover this quarter?' Bazam suggests ideas based on your brand positioning, audience personas, industry trends, and what's previously performed well."

  - q: "Can AI help with content marketing strategy?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Bazam automatically creates a balanced content mix across the buying journey — awareness content for new audiences, educational content for prospects, and conversion content for ready buyers. It maps to the AIDA framework without you needing to understand marketing theory."

  - q: "How do I use AI to create a content calendar?"
    a:
      - "Tell Bazam's agent your themes, goals, or upcoming events for the month. It generates a complete content calendar with posts scheduled across all your platforms — blog, social, everything. You review, adjust, and approve. Your month is planned in one sitting."

  - q: "Can an AI agent onboard new marketing team members?"
    a:
      - "Yes. New team members ask Bazam's agent about any client or brand and get an instant briefing — brand voice, audience personas, campaign history, and content guidelines. The agent replaces weeks of shadowing with minutes of conversation. Client knowledge never walks out the door."

  - q: "How secure is an AI marketing agent?"
    a:
      - "Bazam stores your data securely with encrypted credential storage for all connected platforms. Your brand data, knowledge base, and content are private — never used to train AI models. Role-based access controls ensure team members only see what they should."
