faq:
  - q: "How do agencies scale content production without hiring more writers?"
    a:
      - "Use AI content creation tools. Bazam's wizards let each team member produce the output of 3–5 writers. Brand voice settings ensure quality stays consistent. Scaling means increasing tool leverage, not headcount."

  - q: "How do I price AI-created content for agency clients?"
    a:
      - "Price by deliverable value, not production cost. Your clients pay for strategic, on-brand content — not for the hours it took to create. AI reduces your production cost dramatically while maintaining deliverable quality. Your margins improve."

  - q: "How do I maintain client content quality at scale?"
    a:
      - "Systematic quality controls. Each client project in Bazam has its own brand voice settings (quality floor) and approval workflows (quality gate). Scale doesn't dilute quality when the guardrails are built into the system."

  - q: "How do I report content results to agency clients?"
    a:
      - "Pull analytics per client project. Bazam tracks engagement across each client's connected platforms. Create monthly or quarterly reports showing engagement trends, top-performing content, and platform growth — data tied directly to the content you produced."

  - q: "How do I handle conflicting deadlines across agency clients?"
    a:
      - "Batch-create ahead of deadlines. Use Bazam's content calendar to see all client schedules together. When you spot conflicting deadlines, batch-create the earlier content ahead of time. The calendar makes deadline management visual."

  - q: "How do I onboard agency team members to multiple client accounts?"
    a:
      - "Assign them to client projects in Bazam. Each project has pre-configured brand settings and knowledge base. New team members produce on-brand content immediately — the brand context is in the tool, not in anyone's head."

  - q: "How do I offer content marketing as a new agency service?"
    a:
      - "Start with Bazam as your production engine. Offer blog posts, social media management, email campaigns, and content strategy — all created with AI wizards, managed with the content calendar, and tracked with analytics. The platform provides the capability; you provide the strategy."

  - q: "How do I manage 20+ client accounts efficiently?"
    a:
      - "Each client is an isolated project in Bazam. Switch between clients with a click. Batch-create content per client using wizards. Use the content calendar per project to manage schedules. The platform is designed for multi-client management at scale."

  - q: "How do I demonstrate agency value beyond content creation?"
    a:
      - "Strategy and results. Use Bazam's analytics to show clients what's working and why. Content suggestions demonstrate strategic thinking. The AIDA framework shows purposeful content planning. Your value is strategy and outcomes, not manual writing."

  - q: "How do I retain agency clients with content marketing results?"
    a:
      - "Show consistent improvement. Monthly analytics reports from Bazam demonstrate growing engagement, expanding reach, and increasing follower counts. When clients see measurable progress tied to your content, they stay."
