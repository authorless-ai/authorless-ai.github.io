---
title: "Agency Client Management"
description: "How to manage multiple client accounts, maintain brand consistency, and deliver at scale."
faq:
  - q: "How do I manage content for multiple agency clients?"
    a:
      - "One project per client in Bazam. Each project isolates brand voice, audience personas, knowledge base, content calendar, and channels. Switch between clients instantly. Content never crosses over — even competing clients stay completely separate."

  - q: "How do I maintain each client's brand voice?"
    a:
      - "Define each client's voice in their Bazam project — tone, formality, style, and custom guidelines. The agent enforces these settings for every piece of content. Brand consistency is automatic, regardless of which team member creates the content."

  - q: "How do I onboard new agency clients faster?"
    a:
      - "Upload the client's brand guidelines, product info, and documents to their Bazam project. The agent instantly has full context. New team members ask it 'what's the brand voice for this client?' instead of spending weeks reading scattered briefs."

  - q: "How do I stop client knowledge from being lost when staff leave?"
    a:
      - "Keep client knowledge in Bazam, not in people's heads. Brand voice, audience insights, campaign history, and key documents live in the project. When someone leaves, the knowledge stays. New hires ask the agent and get a full briefing in minutes."

  - q: "How do I report to agency clients efficiently?"
    a:
      - "Ask Bazam's agent for a client performance summary. It pulls engagement, reach, and growth metrics across all the client's channels and presents them in plain English. No more weekly spreadsheet wrangling."

  - q: "How do I handle agency client content requests?"
    a:
      - "Tell the agent. Upload client input to the knowledge base, ask the agent to create the requested content, review, and publish. What used to take days — briefing, writing, revisions — now takes minutes."

  - q: "How do I manage agency client expectations?"
    a:
      - "Deliver faster than they expect. With Bazam handling production, you deliver content the same day it's requested. Clients expect agency timelines; you deliver AI speed. Under-promise, over-deliver becomes the default."

  - q: "How do I ensure content quality across all agency clients?"
    a:
      - "Brand context plus approval workflows. Each client's Bazam project enforces their specific voice, audience, and knowledge base. Approval workflows ensure nothing goes live without review. Quality is systematically enforced, not individually maintained."

  - q: "How do I handle competing agency clients?"
    a:
      - "Project isolation. Bazam keeps every client's data in completely separate projects. Even if two clients are direct competitors, their brand data, content, and knowledge base never cross over. Team members only see the projects they're assigned to."

  - q: "How do I transition agency clients to AI-assisted content?"
    a:
      - "Start with social media content — it's fastest to demonstrate value. Show clients the quality and speed. Then expand to blogs, campaigns, and reporting. Most clients don't care about the method; they care about quality, speed, and results."
