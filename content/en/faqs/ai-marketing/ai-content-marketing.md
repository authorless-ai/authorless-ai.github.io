title: "AI Content Marketing FAQs"
description: "Common questions about using AI for content marketing — how it works, what it costs, and whether it can replace traditional approaches."
faq:
  - q: "What is AI content marketing?"
    a:
      - "AI content marketing uses artificial intelligence to create, schedule, publish, and analyse marketing content. Instead of manually writing every blog post or social update, an AI agent drafts content in your brand voice, suggests topics based on your audience, and handles distribution across multiple platforms."

  - q: "Can AI write content that sounds like my brand?"
    a:
      - "Yes — when it has the right context. Bazam lets you define your brand voice, audience personas, and key messaging. The AI agent uses all of this to produce content that matches your tone, style, and terminology. The more information you provide, the more accurate and on-brand the output becomes."

  - q: "Will Google penalise AI-generated content?"
    a:
      - "Google's guidelines focus on content quality, not how it was produced. AI-generated content that is helpful, accurate, and written for people — not just search engines — is treated the same as human-written content. Bazam's agent creates informed, brand-specific content rather than generic filler, which is exactly what search engines reward."

  - q: "How much does AI content marketing cost compared to hiring a team?"
    a:
      - "A freelance writer typically charges £200–500 per blog post. A marketing agency retainer runs £2,000–10,000 per month. A full-time marketing hire costs £30,000–60,000+ per year. Bazam provides content creation, scheduling, publishing, and analytics for a fraction of any of those options."

  - q: "Can AI replace my marketing team entirely?"
    a:
      - "It depends on your needs. For most small businesses and entrepreneurs, an AI agent can handle the full content production workflow — writing, scheduling, publishing, and reporting. For larger organisations with complex brand requirements, AI works best as a force multiplier alongside your existing team, handling volume while your people focus on strategy and creative direction."

  - q: "What types of content can AI create?"
    a:
      - "Bazam's AI agent can create blog posts, social media updates, press releases, ad copy, email newsletters, product descriptions, and more. Each piece is tailored to the platform it's published on and written in your brand voice."

  - q: "Is AI content marketing suitable for small businesses?"
    a:
      - "Absolutely. Small businesses benefit the most because they typically lack the budget for a full marketing team. An AI agent gives you the output of a marketing department — content creation, scheduling, publishing, and analytics — at a fraction of the cost."

  - q: "How does AI content marketing handle multiple languages?"
    a:
      - "Bazam supports multi-language content creation and translation. You can create content in one language and translate it to others, or create original content in multiple languages simultaneously. This makes it easy to run campaigns in multiple markets without hiring translators."

  - q: "What social media platforms does Bazam support?"
    a:
      - "Bazam publishes directly to WordPress, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X (formerly Twitter), YouTube, and TikTok. You create content once and distribute it across all connected platforms from a single dashboard."

  - q: "How do I get started with AI content marketing?"
    a:
      - "Sign up for Bazam, set up your brand profile — including your brand voice, audience personas, and any reference material in your knowledge base — and start a conversation with your AI agent. Tell it what you need, review the drafts, and approve them for publishing. Most teams are up and running within an hour."
