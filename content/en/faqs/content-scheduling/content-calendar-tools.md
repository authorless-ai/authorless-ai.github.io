  - q: "What is the difference between a content calendar and a scheduling tool?"
    a:
      - "A content calendar is where you plan what to publish and when. A scheduling tool queues it for publishing. Bazam is both — it provides a visual calendar for planning and automated scheduling for publishing. Most tools are one or the other; Bazam is both, plus it creates the content."

  - q: "Do I need a separate tool for content scheduling?"
    a:
      - "Not with Bazam. Most scheduling tools require you to create content somewhere else, then upload and schedule it. Bazam's AI agent creates the content AND handles scheduling and publishing — one tool, end to end."

    a:
      - "For planning — maybe. For execution — no. A spreadsheet doesn't create content, publish it, or track performance. Bazam replaces the spreadsheet with a visual calendar that's connected to your content creation, publishing, and analytics."
