  - q: "How do I balance different content types on my social calendar?"
    a:
      - "Mix educational, entertaining, promotional, and community content. Bazam.ai's suggestions help balance the mix. On the calendar, visually confirm you're not clustering too many promotional posts together — spread them across the month."

  - q: "How do I spot gaps in my social content calendar?"
    a:
      - "Look at the calendar — empty dates are visible immediately. Bazam.ai colour-codes by status, making it obvious where content is missing. Fill gaps with quick wizard-created posts."

  - q: "How do I handle breaking news or trending topics on a planned calendar?"
    a:
      - "Create reactive content quickly with the AI agent, publish immediately, and adjust the existing calendar if needed. Move scheduled content to different slots to make room. The calendar accommodates both planned and reactive publishing."

  - q: "How do I create evergreen social content?"

      - "Tips, how-tos, and helpful advice that stay relevant indefinitely. Create evergreen posts with Bazam.ai's social wizard and mark them for recycling. Periodically refresh and reschedule them on the calendar — filling gaps without creating new content from scratch."
