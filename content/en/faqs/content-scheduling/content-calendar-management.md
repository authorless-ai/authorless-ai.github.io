---
title: "Content Calendar Management"
description: "How to use a visual content calendar to plan, organise, and manage your entire publishing schedule without spreadsheets."
faq:
  - q: "What is a content calendar and do I need one?"
    a:
      - "A content calendar is a visual overview of what content is publishing, where, and when. If you publish content anywhere — blog, social, email — you need one. Without it, you're guessing and scrambling. Bazam's calendar shows every platform, every date, every status in one colour-coded view."

  - q: "How do I set up a content calendar?"
    a:
      - "In Bazam: connect your publishing platforms, create your first batch of content, and schedule it. The calendar populates automatically as you create and schedule content. Switch between monthly, weekly, or daily views. Filter by platform or campaign. No spreadsheets required."

  - q: "How do I use a content calendar to stay consistent?"
    a:
      - "The calendar makes gaps visible. When you see three empty days in a row, you know to fill them. Batch-create content using AI wizards, place it on the calendar, and your schedule is set. Consistency becomes visual and achievable."

  - q: "How do I move content around on my calendar?"
    a:
      - "Drag and drop. Click any piece of scheduled content on Bazam's calendar and drag it to a new date or time. The post updates automatically — no need to re-enter anything. Reschedule quickly when plans change."

  - q: "Can I see what status each piece of content is in?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Bazam's calendar colour-codes content by status — draft, in review, approved, scheduled, published. At a glance you see what's still being worked on, what's waiting for approval, and what's queued to go live."

  - q: "How do I plan content around holiday dates?"
    a:
      - "Mark key dates on the calendar. Work backwards to schedule the lead-up content — teasers, announcements, day-of posts, and follow-up. Bazam's suggestions feature generates campaign ideas when you provide the dates. Plan holiday campaigns months in advance."

  - q: "Is a content calendar better than a spreadsheet?"
    a:
      - "Much better. Spreadsheets don't connect to your publishing platforms, don't auto-publish, don't show status, and can't create content. Bazam's calendar is integrated — content is created, approved, scheduled, and published from the same place. The calendar is the command centre."

  - q: "How do I share my content calendar with my team?"
    a:
      - "Add team members to your Bazam project. Everyone with access sees the same live calendar — no emailing spreadsheets, no version conflicts. Permissions control who can create, edit, approve, or just view the schedule."

  - q: "How do I fill gaps in my content schedule?"
    a:
      - "Look at your calendar and see which days have no content. Use Bazam's content suggestions for quick topic ideas, create the content with a wizard in minutes, and drop it into the empty slots. Filling gaps takes minutes, not hours."

  - q: "How far in advance should I plan my content calendar?"
    a:
      - "4 weeks minimum. Plan a month ahead in one batching session — content suggestions, wizard creation, calendar scheduling. Seasonal campaigns should be planned 2–3 months out. The further ahead you plan, the less scrambling you do."
