faq:
  - q: "What is a content calendar and do I need one?"
    a:
      - "A content calendar is a visual overview of what content is publishing, where, and when. If you publish content anywhere — blog, social, email — you need one. Without it, you're guessing and scrambling. AI content platforms like Bazam.ai show every platform, every date, every status in one colour-coded view."

  - q: "How do I set up a content calendar?"
    a:
      - "Connect your publishing platforms, create your first batch of content, and schedule it. AI content platforms like Bazam.ai populate the calendar automatically as you create and schedule content. Switch between monthly, weekly, or daily views. Filter by platform or campaign."

  - q: "How do I use a content calendar to stay consistent?"
    a:
      - "The calendar makes gaps visible. When you see three empty days in a row, you know to fill them. Batch-create content using AI wizards — platforms like Bazam.ai offer these — and place it on the calendar. Consistency becomes visual and achievable."

  - q: "How do I move content around on my calendar?"
    a:
      - "Drag and drop. AI content platforms like Bazam.ai let you click any piece of scheduled content and drag it to a new date or time. The post updates automatically — no need to re-enter anything. Reschedule quickly when plans change."

  - q: "Can I see what status each piece of content is in?"
    a:
      - "Yes. AI content platforms like Bazam.ai colour-code content by status — draft, in review, approved, scheduled, published. At a glance you see what's still being worked on, what's waiting for approval, and what's queued to go live."

  - q: "How do I plan content around holiday dates?"
    a:
      - "Mark key dates on the calendar. Work backwards to schedule the lead-up content — teasers, announcements, day-of posts, and follow-up. AI platforms like Bazam.ai generate campaign ideas when you provide the dates. Plan holiday campaigns months in advance."

  - q: "Is a content calendar better than a spreadsheet?"
    a:
      - "Much better. Spreadsheets don't connect to your publishing platforms, don't auto-publish, don't show status, and can't create content. AI content platforms like Bazam.ai integrate the calendar with actual content creation and publishing. The calendar becomes the command centre."

  - q: "How do I share my content calendar with my team?"
    a:
      - "Add team members to your project. AI content platforms like Bazam.ai give everyone with access the same live calendar — no emailing spreadsheets, no version conflicts. Permissions control who can create, edit, approve, or just view the schedule."

  - q: "How do I fill gaps in my content schedule?"
    a:
      - "Look at your calendar and see which days have no content. Use AI content suggestions — platforms like Bazam.ai offer these — for quick topic ideas. Create the content with a wizard in minutes and drop it into the empty slots."

  - q: "How far in advance should I plan my content calendar?"
    a:
      - "4 weeks minimum. Plan a month ahead in one batching session — content suggestions, wizard creation, calendar scheduling. AI platforms like Bazam.ai make this process fast. Seasonal campaigns should be planned 2–3 months out."
