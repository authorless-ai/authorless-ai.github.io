faq:
  - q: "What are the biggest content calendar mistakes?"
    a:
      - "Not planning far enough ahead, leaving gaps visible only when it's too late, and over-scheduling without quality review. Bazam.ai's visual calendar makes these problems obvious — colour-coded statuses show what's approved, what's in review, and where the gaps are."

  - q: "How do I make my content calendar actionable?"
    a:
      - "Connect it to your creation and publishing tools. A calendar that's just a spreadsheet creates extra work. Bazam.ai's calendar is integrated — content is created, approved, scheduled, and published from the same platform. The calendar drives action, not just planning."

  - q: "How do I review and update my content calendar weekly?"
    a:
      - "Every Monday, check this week's scheduled content and next week's pipeline. In Bazam.ai, review what's approved and queued, what needs attention, and whether any gaps need filling. A 10-minute weekly review keeps the calendar healthy."

  - q: "How do I colour-code my content calendar?"
    a:
      - "By content status or type. Bazam.ai colour-codes automatically by status — draft, in review, approved, scheduled, published. At a glance you see the health of your pipeline: lots of red (draft) means you need to push more through approval."

  - q: "How do I build a content calendar from scratch?"
    a:
      - "Start with your publishing cadence — how many posts per week, which platforms. Use Bazam.ai's content suggestions to fill the first month with topics. Create content with wizards and place each piece on the calendar. Repeat monthly."

  - q: "How do I use a content calendar for campaign planning?"
    a:
      - "Overlay campaigns on your regular calendar. In Bazam.ai, tag content with campaign names. View the full calendar to see how campaigns interact with regular posting. Ensure campaigns get priority visibility without disrupting consistent publishing."

  - q: "How do I handle content that needs last-minute changes?"
    a:
      - "Edit directly in the calendar. Click any scheduled piece in Bazam.ai, make changes, and the updated version publishes at the original time. Or reschedule by dragging to a new date. Last-minute adjustments take seconds."

  - q: "How do I archive old content calendar entries?"
    a:
      - "Published content automatically moves to a published state in Bazam.ai. Your active calendar always shows current and upcoming content. Past published content is accessible in your content history, not cluttering the active view."

  - q: "How do I sync my content calendar with my team's workflow?"
    a:
      - "Everyone works from the same calendar. In Bazam.ai, all team members see the same live schedule. Content moves through the workflow — draft to review to approved to scheduled — and the calendar reflects each status change in real time."

  - q: "How do I use the content calendar for annual planning?"
    a:
      - "Map the year's key dates, campaigns, and seasons. Mark holidays, product launches, and industry events. Build content strategies around each. Bazam.ai's calendar supports long-range planning — see the full year and plan content themes for each period."
