title: "International Content Localisation"
description: "How to localise content for international markets — beyond translation, adapting messaging for cultural relevance."
faq:
  - q: "What is the difference between translation and localisation?"
    a:
      - "Translation converts words; localisation adapts meaning. Localisation includes cultural references, currency, measurements, humour, and local conventions. Bazam's translation feature goes beyond word-for-word — it adapts content for cultural relevance while preserving brand voice."

  - q: "How do I localise marketing content for European markets?"
    a:
      - "Translate and adapt for each market. GDPR compliance messaging, local currency in pricing, metric measurements, and culturally appropriate imagery references. Bazam handles the translation and cultural adaptation; you review for market-specific accuracy."

  - q: "How do I create location-specific landing pages?"
    a:
      - "Create your master landing page, then create translated and localised versions for each market. Upload market-specific details (pricing, availability, local testimonials) to the knowledge base. Bazam creates locally relevant versions."

  - q: "How do I manage content localisation at scale?"
    a:
      - "Create master content in one language, then translate and localise in batches. Bazam translates into 50+ languages per session. Separate projects per market keep translated content organised. Scale across markets without scaling your team."

  - q: "How do I adapt ad copy for different countries?"
    a:
      - "Translate and culturally adapt — humour, references, and aspirational messaging differ by market. Use Bazam's translation with market-specific brand settings. Each market's ad copy resonates locally while maintaining global brand consistency."

  - q: "How do I create multilingual FAQ pages?"
    a:
      - "Create comprehensive FAQ pages in your primary language, then translate each page into target languages using Bazam's translation. Each language version includes the same JSON-LD structured data — making your FAQs machine-readable in every language."

  - q: "How do I handle languages with different character lengths?"
    a:
      - "German text is typically 30% longer than English; Japanese is more compact. Bazam's translation accounts for these differences — especially important for social media posts with character limits and ad copy with space constraints."

  - q: "How do I maintain SEO across multiple language versions?"
    a:
      - "Each translated page needs its own SEO-structured content — localised keywords, meta descriptions, and headings. Bazam's translation creates complete, SEO-optimised versions for each language, not just translated text but translated search intent."

  - q: "How do I test which markets respond best to my content?"
    a:
      - "Start with 2–3 priority markets. Create and publish translated content. Compare engagement in Bazam's analytics per market project. The data tells you which markets respond best — invest more in high-performing markets."

  - q: "How do I create a global content strategy?"
    a:
      - "Central strategy, localised execution. Define core themes and messaging globally. Translate and adapt for each market using Bazam. Each market has its own project, calendar, and publishing channels — global consistency with local relevance."
