faq:
  - q: "How do I translate a complete marketing campaign?"
    a:
      - "Create the campaign in your primary language, then translate all assets. Bazam translates blog posts, social media, and email content into 50+ languages. Each translation preserves your brand voice while adapting for the target language. Schedule translated content on each market's calendar."

  - q: "How do I coordinate content across multiple language markets?"
    a:
      - "Separate projects for each market. Create content in the primary language, translate, and distribute to market-specific projects. Each project has its own calendar and publishing channels. Manage global campaigns from one account with clean market isolation."

  - q: "How do I ensure marketing translations are accurate?"
    a:
      - "AI translation with brand context produces better results than generic translation tools. Bazam's translations are informed by your brand voice settings — maintaining tone, formality, and messaging. For critical campaigns, have a native speaker review before publishing."

  - q: "How do I adapt social media campaigns for different countries?"
    a:
      - "Translate the content and adjust regional references — currency, timing, cultural nuances. Create market-specific social accounts connected to market-specific projects in Bazam. Each market's content is tailored and published independently."

  - q: "How do I manage a multilingual blog?"
    a:
      - "Create blog content in your primary language, translate with Bazam, and publish translated versions to language-specific blog pages or subdomains. Each language version gets its own SEO-optimised structure."

  - q: "How do I translate product descriptions for international e-commerce?"
    a:
      - "Upload product catalogues to the knowledge base. Create descriptions in your primary language, then translate with Bazam. Each translation adapts product details for the target market — currency, sizing, and localised terminology."

  - q: "How do I handle cultural differences in translated marketing?"
    a:
      - "Bazam's translation adapts content culturally, not just linguistically. Seasonal references, humour, formality levels, and cultural expectations are adjusted. For highly culture-sensitive campaigns, add market-specific guidelines to the brand settings."

  - q: "How do I publish translated content to different social accounts?"
    a:
      - "Connect market-specific social accounts to market-specific projects. Translated English content goes to the UK account; translated Spanish content goes to the Spain account. Each project publishes to its own connected channels."

  - q: "How many languages should I translate my marketing into?"
    a:
      - "Start with your largest untapped markets. If 20% of your website traffic comes from Spanish-speaking countries, translate to Spanish first. Add languages as the ROI justifies. Bazam makes adding each new language minimal effort."

  - q: "How do I measure which translated markets perform best?"
    a:
      - "Compare analytics per market project. Each market's project in Bazam shows engagement from that market's channels. Compare engagement rates across markets to see where translated content resonates most and where to invest more."
