  - q: "How do I stop AI from writing generic content?"
    a:
      - "Give it context. Generic AI content comes from generic tools that know nothing about your brand. Bazam's agent works from your brand voice settings, audience personas, and knowledge base — so every piece of content is specific to your business, not boilerplate."

  - q: "How do I make AI content more accurate?"
    a:
      - "Upload your source material. Bazam's knowledge base holds product docs, specifications, case studies, and brand guidelines. The agent references these directly when writing, so facts, figures, and claims match your actual materials. No hallucinating specs or making up statistics."

  - q: "Is AI-generated content detectable?"
    a:
      - "Generic AI content is detectable because it sounds generic. Content created with Bazam — using your brand voice, audience data, and product knowledge — reads like professional marketing copy because it IS professional marketing copy, just produced faster. Detection tools flag patterns, not quality."

  - q: "Will Google penalise AI-generated content?"
    a:
      - "No. Google's guidelines focus on content quality, not authorship method. Content that is helpful, accurate, and written for people ranks well regardless of how it was produced. Bazam creates informed, brand-specific content — exactly what search engines reward."

  - q: "How do I ensure AI content matches my brand voice?"
    a:
      - "Define your voice in Bazam — tone, formality, enthusiasm, directness, and custom guidelines. Upload examples of your best content. The agent applies these settings to every piece it creates. The result is consistent brand voice whether one person or ten are creating content."

  - q: "Can AI write content that doesn't sound like AI?"
    a:
      - "Yes — when it has the right context. Bazam's agent creates content using your specific brand voice, product knowledge, and audience understanding. The output sounds like your brand because it IS informed by your brand. Generic chatbots produce generic content; a contextual agent produces professional copy."

  - q: "How do I fact-check AI content?"
    a:
      - "When the AI works from your knowledge base, fact-checking is straightforward — the agent is referencing your own materials. Bazam's workflow includes a review and approval step before publishing, so you always verify content against your source material before it goes live."

  - q: "How do I improve AI content quality over time?"
    a:
      - "Feed Bazam more context. Add new products, case studies, customer testimonials, and competitive research to your knowledge base. Refine your brand voice settings. Update your audience personas as you learn more. The more context the agent has, the better every piece of content becomes."

  - q: "Does AI content get worse when you create a lot of it?"
    a:
      - "Not with Bazam. Each piece is generated from your unique brand context — voice, audience, knowledge base — not from a template. Whether you create 5 pieces or 500, the agent produces individually tailored content. Quality is a function of context, not volume."

  - q: "How do I review AI content before publishing?"
    a:
      - "Bazam includes a built-in approval workflow. Content moves from draft to review to approved to published. Nothing goes live without someone on your team signing off. You can edit any piece directly within the platform before approving it."
