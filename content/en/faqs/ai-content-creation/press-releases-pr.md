  - q: "When should I write a press release?"
    a:

  - q: "How do I create a press release for a product launch?"
    a:
      - "Lead with the customer benefit, not the product features. Upload product details and target market information to the knowledge base. Bazam creates a news-focused release that journalists can use directly — not a marketing brochure in press release format."

  - q: "How do I create a media kit for my company?"
