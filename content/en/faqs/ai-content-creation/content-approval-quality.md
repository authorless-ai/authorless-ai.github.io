---
title: "Content Approval & Quality Control"
description: "How to maintain content quality when multiple people create content — review workflows, sign-off processes, and quality standards."
faq:
  - q: "How do I make sure content is reviewed before it goes live?"
    a:
      - "Set up an approval workflow. Bazam routes content through designated reviewers before it can be published or scheduled. Contributors create drafts, approvers get notified, review in-platform, and either approve or request changes. Nothing goes live without sign-off."

  - q: "How do I maintain quality when my team creates content?"
    a:
      - "Two controls: brand voice settings and approval workflows. Bazam enforces your brand voice automatically on every piece — regardless of who creates it. Then your approval process catches anything that needs adjustment before it reaches your audience."

  - q: "How do I stop junior staff from publishing low-quality content?"
    a:
      - "Require approval before publishing. In Bazam, content from contributor-level users automatically queues for editor or admin review. Junior staff create freely — the AI handles the writing quality — but nothing publishes without a senior's sign-off."

  - q: "What is a content approval workflow?"
    a:
      - "A defined review process for content before publication. In Bazam: content is created → submitted for review → approved or returned with feedback → then scheduled or published. Every step is tracked with timestamps and who performed each action."

  - q: "How do I give feedback on content drafts efficiently?"
    a:
      - "Review drafts in the same platform where they're created. Bazam's approval queue lets reviewers add inline feedback, request changes, or approve — all in one place. No email threads, no separate documents, no lost comments."

  - q: "Is there a way to track who approved what content?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Bazam maintains a full audit trail — who created each piece, who reviewed it, who approved it, and when each action happened. Useful for accountability, client reporting, and compliance requirements."

  - q: "How do I approve content when I'm on holiday?"
    a:
      - "Delegate approval to another authorised team member. In Bazam, admins can reassign approval responsibilities so content doesn't get stuck. Alternatively, batch-approve before you leave — review and sign off a week of scheduled content in one session."

  - q: "How do agencies manage content approval for multiple clients?"
    a:
      - "Each client project has its own approval workflow in Bazam. Account managers create content, internal reviewers approve it, and optionally clients can review before scheduling. Separate workflows per project keep client content isolated and quality-controlled."

  - q: "How do I review a large batch of content at once?"
    a:
      - "Use Bazam's approval queue. All pending content appears in a single review list. Work through the queue, approving or requesting changes on each piece. Batch review is especially useful after content batching sessions or when returning from time away."

  - q: "Does content approval slow down publishing?"
    a:
      - "Not if you batch your workflow. Create content in advance, route through approval, then schedule. By the time scheduled dates arrive, everything is already approved. Bazam's AI produces on-brand first drafts, so approval is usually a quick review, not a rewrite."
