---
title: "Setting Up Your Brand Voice for AI Content"
description: "How to configure brand voice settings so every piece of AI-generated content sounds like your business."
faq:
  - q: "How do I make AI content sound like my brand?"
    a:
      - "Define your brand voice settings before creating content. In Bazam.ai, configure your tone (professional, casual, witty), formality level, personality traits, and any words or phrases to use or avoid. Every piece of content — from the wizards and the agent — automatically follows these settings."

  - q: "How do I analyse my existing brand voice?"
    a:
      - "Upload samples of your best existing content. Bazam.ai's brand analysis tool examines your writing samples and extracts your tone, style, vocabulary, and personality traits. It creates a brand voice profile from your existing content — so the AI writes like you, not like a generic robot."

  - q: "Why does my AI content sound generic?"
    a:
      - "Because it doesn't have enough context about your brand. Generic AI tools produce generic content. Bazam.ai uses your brand voice settings, audience personas, and uploaded knowledge base documents to produce informed, specific content. The more context you give it, the more it sounds like you."

  - q: "How do I keep my brand voice consistent across all content?"
    a:
      - "Set it once and enforce it everywhere. Bazam.ai applies your brand voice settings to every piece of content regardless of who creates it, which wizard they use, or what platform they target. Consistency is built into the system, not left to individual judgment."

  - q: "How do I write in my CEO's voice?"
    a:
      - "Upload examples of the CEO's writing and use Bazam.ai's brand analysis to capture their personal style. Create a personality profile — communication style, topics of expertise, tone preferences. Content created for the CEO follows that specific voice profile."

  - q: "How do I set up audience personas for better content targeting?"
    a:
      - "Define your target audiences in Bazam.ai with demographics, motivations, pain points, and buying behaviour. When creating content, the AI tailors messaging to these personas — addressing their specific interests and concerns rather than speaking to everyone generically."

  - q: "Can I have different brand voices for different products?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Create separate projects in Bazam.ai for each brand or product line. Each project has its own voice settings, audience personas, and knowledge base. Content for Product A never bleeds into Product B's voice — complete isolation."

  - q: "How do I update my brand voice as my business evolves?"
    a:
      - "Edit your brand voice settings in Bazam.ai at any time. Re-run the brand analysis with new content samples, adjust tone parameters, or update your custom guidelines. All future content immediately reflects the updated settings."

  - q: "How do I train AI to match my brand's unique terminology?"
    a:
      - "Add custom terms and phrases to your brand settings. Bazam.ai includes glossaries and terminology preferences in your voice profile — industry jargon, product names, and proprietary terms are used correctly in every piece of content."

  - q: "What is the difference between brand voice and brand tone?"
    a:
      - "Voice is your brand's personality — it stays consistent. Tone adapts to context — you might be authoritative in a whitepaper and friendly on social media. Bazam.ai's brand settings handle both — consistent personality with tone that adjusts per content type and platform."
