---
title: "Brand Analysis & Discovery"
description: "How to analyse and discover your brand identity — extracting voice, tone, and personality from existing content and raw inputs."
faq:
  - q: "How do I discover my brand voice?"
    a:
      - "Analyse your existing content. Bazam.ai's brand analysis tool examines your website, social media, and published materials to extract your current voice — tone, formality, vocabulary, and personality traits. Instead of guessing your voice, discover it from data."

  - q: "How do I analyse my brand's current content?"
    a:
      - "Point Bazam.ai's brand analysis at your website URL. The AI analyses your published content and extracts patterns — how you communicate, what tone you use, which words appear frequently, and what personality your content projects."

  - q: "How do I define my brand's personality?"
    a:
      - "Choose personality traits that match your ideal perception. Professional or casual? Authoritative or approachable? Serious or playful? Bazam.ai lets you define multiple personality traits. Every piece of content then reflects this defined personality."

  - q: "How do I audit my brand voice for consistency?"
    a:
      - "Run brand analysis on your recent content. Compare the extracted voice against your defined brand settings. Discrepancies reveal where your content is drifting from the defined voice — fix the content or update the settings."

  - q: "How do I create a brand voice for a new company?"
    a:
      - "Start with your values, audience, and positioning. In Bazam.ai, define your personality traits, tone preferences, and communication style. The AI creates content from these settings from day one — your brand voice is established through published content."

  - q: "How do I evolve my brand voice over time?"
    a:
      - "Update your brand settings in Bazam.ai. Changes apply to all future content immediately. Existing published content stays as-is. A gradual voice evolution feels natural when new content shifts tone while maintaining core personality."

  - q: "How do I create different brand voices for sub-brands?"
    a:
      - "Separate projects for each sub-brand. Each project has independent voice settings — different tone, personality, and audience targeting. The parent brand personality can be shared while sub-brands express their unique identity."

  - q: "How do I communicate my brand voice to my team?"
    a:
      - "Don't rely on documents — embed it in the tools. Bazam.ai's brand voice settings ARE the style guide. When team members create content through wizards, the voice is applied automatically. No interpretation required."

  - q: "How do I measure whether my brand voice is resonating?"
    a:
      - "Track engagement on-brand content versus off-brand content. Bazam.ai's analytics show which voice characteristics drive the most engagement. If more formal content outperforms casual content, your audience is telling you what they prefer."

  - q: "How do I capture competitor brand voice insights?"
    a:
      - "Use Bazam.ai's brand analysis tool on competitor websites. Extract their voice patterns — then deliberately differentiate. If competitors all sound corporate, be approachable. If they're all casual, be authoritative. Analysis informs differentiation."
