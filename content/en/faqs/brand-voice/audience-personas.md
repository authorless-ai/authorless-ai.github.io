title: "Audience Personas & Targeting"
description: "How to define audience personas and use them to create targeted, relevant marketing content."
faq:
  - q: "What is an audience persona?"
    a:
      - "An audience persona is a detailed profile of your ideal customer — demographics, motivations, pain points, buying behaviour, and communication preferences. Bazam lets you build rich personas including psychographics, emotional drivers, expertise levels, and personality traits. Every piece of content the agent creates speaks to these personas."

  - q: "How do I create audience personas?"
    a:
      - "In Bazam, describe your target audience and the AI analyses it to build a detailed persona — demographics, psychographics, buying behaviour, emotional drivers, and motivations. You can create multiple personas for different segments and the agent tailors content to each."

  - q: "How many audience personas do I need?"
    a:
      - "Most businesses need 2–5 personas covering their key customer segments. Bazam lets you create as many as needed. More is fine if your segments are genuinely different — the agent tailors content to whichever persona you target with each piece."

  - q: "How do audience personas improve content marketing?"
    a:
      - "Content written for 'everyone' resonates with no one. When Bazam's agent creates content using your persona's specific pain points, desires, and language, the output speaks directly to them. Targeted content consistently outperforms generic content in engagement and conversions."

  - q: "How do I use audience personas with AI content?"
    a:
      - "Define your personas in Bazam once. When creating content, the agent automatically considers your audience's motivations, expertise level, and preferences. The result is content that speaks to your customers' actual needs — not generic marketing filler."

  - q: "What information should I include in an audience persona?"
    a:
      - "The more detail, the better. Bazam supports demographics, psychographics, interests, values, emotional drivers, buying behaviour, personality traits, expertise levels, and primary motivations. Rich personas produce rich, targeted content."

  - q: "How do I create content for different audience segments?"
    a:
      - "Define separate personas in Bazam for each segment. When creating content, target the relevant persona. The agent adjusts messaging, tone, and focus for each audience. Same brand voice, different audience emphasis."

  - q: "Can AI generate audience personas automatically?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Bazam's AI analyses your audience descriptions to build detailed personas. Provide a brief description of your target customer and the agent generates demographics, psychographics, buying behaviour, emotional drivers, and motivations — which you can then refine."

  - q: "How often should I update my audience personas?"
    a:
      - "Review quarterly. As your business evolves, your understanding of your audience deepens. Update personas in Bazam as you learn more about your customers' motivations and behaviour. The agent immediately adapts all future content."

  - q: "How do I make content that resonates with my target audience?"
    a:
      - "Know them intimately. Define detailed personas in Bazam — not just demographics, but motivations, fears, and desires. The agent creates content that addresses what your audience actually cares about. Resonance comes from relevance, and relevance comes from understanding."
