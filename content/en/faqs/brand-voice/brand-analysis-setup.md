---
title: "Brand Analysis & Setup"
description: "How to set up your brand in a marketing platform — automated analysis, brand hierarchy, and personal brand support."
faq:
  - q: "How do I set up my brand in a marketing tool?"
    a:
      - "Bazam automates it. Point it at your website and the AI analyses your company details, brand positioning, offerings, target audiences, and competitive landscape. You review the results, adjust if needed, and your brand profile is ready — in minutes, not hours."

  - q: "What is automated brand analysis?"
    a:
      - "Bazam's AI analyses your website to automatically extract your company details, products/services, brand positioning, target audiences, and competitive landscape. It's like having a branding consultant do a discovery session — except it takes minutes and costs nothing."

  - q: "How long does it take to set up a brand in Bazam?"
    a:
      - "Minutes. The automated brand analysis extracts your key information from your website. You review and refine the results, set your voice parameters, and upload key documents. Most brands are fully set up in under an hour."

  - q: "Can I manage multiple brands from one account?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Bazam supports a Company → Brand → Offering hierarchy. Manage multiple brands under one organisation, each with their own voice, audiences, and content strategy. Agencies manage dozens of client brands from a single account."

  - q: "What is the Company → Brand → Offering hierarchy?"
    a:
      - "It's how Bazam structures businesses. Your company has one or more brands, each brand has one or more offerings (products/services). This hierarchy lets you create content that's specific to each product while maintaining brand-level consistency."

  - q: "Can I use AI for personal branding?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Bazam has a personality/influencer mode designed for personal brands and thought leaders. Define your voice, expertise, and audience — the agent creates content that sounds like you, builds your authority, and maintains your authentic perspective."

  - q: "How does AI learn about my business?"
    a:
      - "Three ways in Bazam: automated website analysis extracts your positioning and offerings, you define audience personas and voice settings, and you upload key documents to the knowledge base. Combined, the agent has a deep understanding of your business."

  - q: "Can I change my brand settings after setup?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Brand settings in Bazam are fully editable at any time. Adjust your voice, update audience personas, add knowledge base documents, or refine your brand positioning. All future content automatically reflects the changes."

  - q: "How do I differentiate between brands within my company?"
    a:
      - "Give each brand its own profile in Bazam with distinct voice settings, audience personas, and knowledge base. The agent creates content that's unique to each brand — even if they're under the same company umbrella."

  - q: "Can franchises use AI to maintain brand consistency across locations?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Set up the master brand in Bazam with voice guidelines and approved messaging. Each location can create content through the agent, which enforces the franchise's brand standards automatically. Corporate brand consistency with local relevance."
