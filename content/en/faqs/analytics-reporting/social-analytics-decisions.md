faq:
  - q: "How do I turn analytics into better content decisions?"
    a:
      - "Identify patterns. AI marketing platforms like Bazam.ai show which topics, formats, and posting times drive the best engagement. Use these patterns to inform next month's content — more of what works, less of what doesn't. Data replaces guesswork."

  - q: "How do I identify my best content topics from analytics?"
    a:
      - "Sort by engagement in your analytics dashboard. In platforms like Bazam.ai, your top-performing posts cluster around specific themes. Those themes are what your audience cares about most. Create more content on those themes using AI wizards."

  - q: "How do I know which social platform to invest more time in?"
    a:
      - "Compare engagement rates across platforms. AI marketing platforms like Bazam.ai show platform-by-platform analytics. The platform with the highest engagement rate (not just follower count) is where your content resonates most. Invest more there; experiment or reduce on underperforming channels."

  - q: "What does a drop in social engagement mean?"
    a:
      - "Possible causes: posting frequency change, content quality shift, audience fatigue, or algorithm change. AI platforms like Bazam.ai help you check the timing — did the drop coincide with a content change? The data narrows the cause."

  - q: "How do I use analytics to find my best posting times?"
    a:
      - "Review engagement patterns by day and time in your analytics. AI platforms like Bazam.ai track when your posts get the most interaction. Over time, clear patterns emerge — use those patterns to schedule future content at peak engagement times."

  - q: "How do I track the impact of changes to my content strategy?"
    a:
      - "Compare before and after in analytics. When you shift strategy — new content types, different posting frequency, new platforms — track engagement trends with a platform like Bazam.ai from that date. Improvement (or decline) after the change tells you whether the shift is working."

  - q: "How do I use analytics to prove my content is reaching the right people?"
    a:
      - "Track engagement quality, not just quantity. Comments, shares, and saves indicate deeper interest than likes alone. AI marketing platforms like Bazam.ai show engagement breakdowns. If your target audience is engaging — commenting, sharing, clicking — you're reaching the right people."

  - q: "When should I pivot my social media strategy based on data?"
    a:
      - "When 3+ months of data show declining engagement despite consistent publishing. Short-term dips are normal; sustained decline means something needs to change. AI platforms like Bazam.ai make this distinction clear in their trend views over time."

  - q: "How do I create a data-driven social media strategy?"
    a:
      - "Start with 3 months of consistent publishing and analytics collection. Use an AI platform like Bazam.ai to identify what works — best topics, formats, times, and platforms. Build your strategy around the data. Reassess quarterly as new data accumulates."

  - q: "How do I track competitor social media performance?"
    a:
      - "Monitor their public engagement manually — followers, likes, comments, shares. Note which content types earn their best engagement. Use those insights to inform your own strategy — but don't copy. AI platforms like Bazam.ai help you create better content on the same themes, tailored to your brand voice."
