---
title: "Marketing Attribution & Goal Tracking"
description: "How to attribute business results to content marketing efforts and track progress toward marketing goals."
faq:
  - q: "How do I attribute leads to content marketing?"
    a:
      - "Use UTM parameters on your content links. Bazam supports UTM tracking at the campaign level, so you can trace which content pieces and campaigns drive traffic, leads, and conversions. Connect the dots between content and business results."

  - q: "How do I set marketing goals?"
    a:
      - "Define your goals in Bazam — brand awareness, engagement growth, lead generation, or conversions. The agent creates content aligned to these goals and tracks progress. Start with one primary goal and expand as you see results."

  - q: "How do I know if my content marketing is working?"
    a:
      - "Track engagement trends, not individual post performance. If your engagement, reach, and follower growth are consistently improving month over month, your strategy is working. Bazam shows these trends clearly in your analytics dashboard."

  - q: "How do I measure brand awareness from content?"
    a:
      - "Track reach, impressions, and follower growth. Bazam aggregates these metrics across all platforms. Growing reach and followers indicate increasing brand awareness. The agent summarises awareness trends over time."

  - q: "How do I calculate cost per content piece?"
    a:
      - "Divide your Bazam subscription cost by the number of pieces created. Most businesses create 30–100+ pieces per month, making the cost per piece dramatically lower than freelancers (£200–500/piece) or agencies (even higher per piece at retainer rates)."

  - q: "How do I measure content marketing ROI for my boss?"
    a:
      - "Show three things: reduced production costs (AI vs previous methods), increased output (pieces per month), and improved engagement/traffic metrics. Bazam provides all three data points. The ROI case is usually overwhelming."

  - q: "What is a good content marketing KPI?"
    a:
      - "Engagement rate (are people interacting?), reach growth (are more people seeing your content?), traffic (are people visiting your site?), and conversion rate (are they taking action?). Bazam tracks the first three automatically; pair with Google Analytics for conversions."

  - q: "How do I report content marketing value to stakeholders?"
    a:
      - "Generate a performance summary from Bazam and highlight the business impact — engagement growth, reach expansion, and traffic driven by content. Translate metrics into business language that stakeholders understand."

  - q: "How do I track content performance over longer periods?"
    a:
      - "Bazam stores historical analytics data so you can track performance over weeks, months, and quarters. Long-term trends are more meaningful than daily fluctuations. Ask the agent for a quarterly summary to see the big picture."

  - q: "How do I know when to change my content strategy?"
    a:
      - "When metrics plateau or decline for 4–6 weeks despite consistent effort. Bazam's analytics show this clearly. Ask the agent what's changed and it recommends adjustments — new topics, different formats, or adjusted targeting."
