title: "Social Media Analytics & Performance Tracking"
description: "How to track social media performance across all platforms and understand what's working."
faq:
  - q: "How do I track social media analytics across all platforms?"
    a:
      - "Bazam.ai aggregates analytics from all connected platforms — Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, TikTok — into a single dashboard. See engagement, reach, impressions, clicks, and follower growth in one view instead of logging into each platform separately."

  - q: "What social media metrics should I track?"
    a:
      - "Engagement (likes, comments, shares), reach (impressions, unique viewers), growth (follower count over time), clicks (link clicks, profile visits), and content performance (top posts, worst performers). Bazam.ai tracks all of these automatically."

  - q: "How do I see which social media posts are performing best?"
    a:
      - "Bazam.ai ranks your content by engagement metrics — likes, comments, shares, clicks, and reach. Ask the agent 'what content performed best this week?' and get a plain-English summary identifying your top performers and why they resonated."

  - q: "How do I track follower growth over time?"
    a:
      - "Bazam.ai tracks follower counts across all connected platforms and shows growth trends over time. See which campaigns and content types drive the most follower growth, and adjust your strategy accordingly."

  - q: "How do I track engagement rate on social media?"
    a:
      - "Bazam.ai calculates engagement rates automatically — total engagements divided by reach. Track rates per post, per platform, and over time. Identify which content types consistently drive the highest engagement."

  - q: "How do I measure reach vs impressions?"
    a:
      - "Reach is unique viewers; impressions is total views (one person can generate multiple impressions). Bazam.ai tracks both. High impressions with low reach means your audience is seeing content repeatedly. High reach with low engagement means you need better targeting."

  - q: "How do I compare performance across different social platforms?"
    a:
      - "Bazam.ai's dashboard shows metrics side by side for all connected platforms. Compare engagement rates, follower growth, and content performance across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, and TikTok in one view."

  - q: "How do I track content performance for a specific campaign?"
    a:
      - "Tag content with campaigns in Bazam.ai. Track engagement, reach, and growth for all content within a campaign. Ask the agent for a campaign performance summary and get a clear picture of what's working."

  - q: "How do I identify my best-performing content topics?"
    a:
      - "Bazam.ai analyses engagement data across all your content and identifies patterns — which topics, formats, and posting times drive the best results. Ask the agent what's working and it summarises your top performers."

  - q: "What does good social media performance look like?"
    a:
      - "It depends on your baseline. More important than industry benchmarks is whether you're improving. Bazam.ai tracks your metrics over time so you can see growth trends. Consistent improvement signals that your content strategy is working."
