---
title: "Using Data to Improve Content Marketing"
description: "How to make data-driven content decisions — which metrics to track, how to interpret them, and how to act on insights."
faq:
  - q: "How do I use data to make better content decisions?"
    a:
      - "Track what performs, identify patterns, create more of what works. Bazam's analytics show engagement by content type, topic, and platform. When data shows your how-to posts get 3x more engagement than news posts, create more how-tos. Let data guide your content mix."

  - q: "Which content metrics actually matter?"
    a:
      - "Engagement rate (not just raw numbers), click-through rate, and follower growth. These tell you whether people care about your content and whether your audience is growing. Bazam tracks all three across every connected platform."

  - q: "How do I A/B test content on social media?"
    a:
      - "Create two versions of the same topic with different angles, headlines, or formats. Schedule both using Bazam and compare engagement in the analytics. The version that performs better tells you what your audience prefers."

  - q: "How do I identify my best-performing content?"
    a:
      - "Sort by engagement in Bazam's analytics. See your top posts across all platforms — which topics, formats, and platforms drive the most interaction. Study the patterns and replicate what works."

  - q: "How do I track which social platform drives the most engagement?"
    a:
      - "Compare platform analytics side by side. Bazam shows engagement rates per channel — not just total numbers, but engagement relative to audience size. The platform with the highest engagement rate is where your content resonates most."

  - q: "How do I optimise content based on performance data?"
    a:
      - "Review analytics monthly. Identify top-performing topics, formats, and posting times. Create next month's content emphasising what worked. Use Bazam's suggestions to generate similar topics, create with wizards, and schedule more confidently."

  - q: "How do I tell if my content frequency is right?"
    a:
      - "Watch your engagement rate as you increase or decrease frequency. If engagement per post stays consistent as you post more, frequency is fine. If per-post engagement drops, you may be posting too often or the quality is diluting. Analytics tell you your sweet spot."

  - q: "How do I create a data-driven content calendar?"
    a:
      - "Fill your calendar based on what the data says works. Bazam's analytics reveal your best topics, formats, and posting times. Use those insights to plan next month — scheduling more of what performs, experimenting with variations, and dropping what doesn't resonate."

  - q: "How do I report content performance to stakeholders?"
    a:
      - "Pull key metrics from Bazam's analytics — engagement trends, top posts, follower growth, platform comparisons. Present month-over-month trends to show progress. Focus on the metrics that tie to business objectives — traffic, leads, brand awareness."

  - q: "How do I spot declining content performance early?"
    a:
      - "Check analytics weekly. Bazam shows engagement trends over time. If engagement is declining week over week, something is changing — content quality, posting frequency, or audience interest. Catch the decline early and adjust before it compounds."
