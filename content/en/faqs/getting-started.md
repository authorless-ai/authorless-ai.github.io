---
title: "Getting Started with Bazam"
description: "Everything you need to know about setting up and using Bazam — from account creation to publishing your first content."
faq:
  - q: "How do I create a Bazam account?"
    a:
      - "Visit bazam.ai and click Sign Up. You can create an account with your email address. Once registered, you'll be guided through setting up your first brand profile."

  - q: "What do I need to set up before creating content?"
    a:
      - "Start by creating a brand profile. This includes your brand voice (tone, style, key phrases), audience personas (who you're writing for), and optionally uploading documents to your knowledge base. The more context you provide, the better your AI agent's output will be."

  - q: "How does the knowledge base work?"
    a:
      - "The knowledge base is where you store reference material your AI agent draws on when creating content. You can upload documents, paste text, or import URLs. This might include product information, company history, industry research, or competitor analysis. The agent uses this information to create accurate, informed content."

  - q: "Can I connect my social media accounts?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Bazam integrates directly with Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, TikTok, and WordPress. Connect your accounts from the settings page, and the agent can publish content directly to any or all of them."

  - q: "How do I create my first piece of content?"
    a:
      - "Start a conversation with your AI agent and tell it what you need — for example, 'write a blog post about our new product launch' or 'create a week of social media posts about sustainability'. The agent will draft content based on your brand profile and knowledge base. Review, edit if needed, and approve for publishing."

  - q: "Can I schedule content in advance?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Bazam includes a visual content calendar where you can schedule posts days, weeks, or months in advance. You can also ask the agent to generate and schedule an entire month of content in one session."

  - q: "How do I track how my content is performing?"
    a:
      - "Bazam has a built-in analytics dashboard that tracks engagement, reach, clicks, and follower growth across all connected platforms. You can also ask the agent for a plain-English summary of your performance over any time period."

  - q: "Can I use Bazam for multiple brands or projects?"
    a:
      - "Yes. Bazam supports multiple brands and projects from a single account. Each brand has its own voice, audience, knowledge base, and connected platforms. This is particularly useful for agencies managing multiple clients."

  - q: "Is there a free trial?"
    a:
      - "Visit our pricing page for the latest information on plans and trial availability. We regularly update our offerings to make Bazam accessible to teams of all sizes."

  - q: "Where can I get help if I'm stuck?"
    a:
      - "You can contact our support team through the contact page on our website. We also have documentation available to help you get the most out of Bazam."
