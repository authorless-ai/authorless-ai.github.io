  - q: "How do I scale content production without scaling my team?"
    a:
      - "Bazam. Each team member's output multiplies with the AI agent. One person produces what previously required two or three. Scale your content volume without proportionally scaling your headcount. The agent handles production; your team handles quality."

    a:
      - "Start everyone in Bazam. Brand voice and knowledge base are already defined. New team members immediately produce on-brand content through the agent. The platform scales with you — one person or fifty, the workflow is the same."

    a:
      - "Two mechanisms: brand context and approval workflows. Bazam's agent produces consistent quality because it works from your defined voice and knowledge base. Approval workflows catch anything that needs adjustment before publishing."

  - q: "How do I reduce the cost per content piece?"
    a:
      - "Use AI for production. Freelancers charge £200–500 per blog post. Agencies charge more. Bazam's agent creates unlimited content for a flat subscription. Your cost per piece drops as volume increases — the opposite of human-powered production."

  - q: "How do I handle high-volume content demand?"
    a:
      - "Bazam scales effortlessly. The AI agent creates content at whatever volume you need — 10 pieces or 500. There's no queue, no waiting list, no capacity constraint. Your production capacity is limited only by review bandwidth."

  - q: "How do I standardise content production across teams?"
    a:
      - "Use Bazam as the single content creation platform for all teams. Brand voice settings, knowledge base, and approval workflows ensure every team produces to the same standard. The AI enforces consistency that style guides can't."

  - q: "How do I measure team content productivity?"
    a:
      - "Track pieces created per team member, approval rates, publishing velocity, and engagement metrics per person. Bazam tracks content lifecycle from creation through performance, giving you visibility into both output and impact."

  - q: "How do I handle content production during busy periods?"
    a:
      - "Create content in advance. Bazam lets you batch-create weeks of content in a single session. During busy periods, your scheduled content runs on autopilot while your team focuses on urgent priorities."

  - q: "What is the most efficient content creation workflow?"
    a:
      - "Agent creates → human reviews → approve → schedule → autopublish. With Bazam, this takes minutes per piece. The most efficient workflow is one where humans review, not write. AI handles the heavy lifting."

  - q: "How do I transition my team from manual content creation to AI?"
