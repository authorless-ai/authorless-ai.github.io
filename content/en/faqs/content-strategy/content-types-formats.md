description: "Which content types and formats work best for different marketing goals — and how to create them efficiently."
faq:
  - q: "What types of content should I be creating?"
    a:
      - "A mix that serves different purposes. Blog posts drive organic search. Social media builds engagement and awareness. Email newsletters nurture leads. Press releases build credibility. Bazam creates all of these from a single platform, automatically adapting format and tone for each."

  - q: "Should I focus on blog posts or social media?"
    a:
      - "Both — they serve complementary purposes. Blog content drives organic search traffic and builds authority. Social media builds engagement, awareness, and community. Bazam creates both simultaneously and even turns blog posts into social promotion content."

  - q: "What is evergreen content and should I create it?"
    a:
      - "Evergreen content stays relevant over time — how-to guides, FAQs, best practices, and reference articles. Absolutely create it. Bazam's agent generates evergreen content that drives traffic for months or years without needing updates. Pair it with timely content for a balanced strategy."

  - q: "How do I create pillar content for my website?"
    a:
      - "Identify your core topics and ask Bazam's agent to create comprehensive pillar pages — in-depth guides covering all aspects of a topic. Then create cluster content — blog posts, FAQs, and social content — that links back to each pillar. The agent handles both."

  - q: "What is the best format for B2B content?"
    a:
      - "Long-form blog posts, LinkedIn articles, case studies, and whitepaper-style guides. B2B audiences want substance and expertise. Bazam creates detailed, insight-driven content from your knowledge base and audience personas — the kind that builds trust with business buyers."

  - q: "What is the best format for B2C content?"
    a:
      - "Social media posts, short-form video scripts, product highlights, and lifestyle content. B2C audiences want engagement, emotion, and relatability. Bazam creates platform-native content that speaks to consumer motivations and buying triggers."

  - q: "How do I decide between short-form and long-form content?"
    a:
      - "Both. Short-form for social engagement and discovery. Long-form for SEO, authority, and depth. Bazam creates both — the agent writes a detailed blog post AND creates short-form social posts from the same topic, covering both bases effortlessly."

  - q: "Should I create video content or written content?"
    a:
      - "Both — they reach different audiences on different platforms. Written content drives search traffic. Video drives social engagement. Bazam creates scripts for video content alongside written pieces, so you can cover both formats from a single session."

  - q: "How do I create content for email newsletters?"
    a:
      - "Ask Bazam's agent to write email newsletters — subject lines, body copy, and calls-to-action. The agent draws on your brand voice and audience data to create emails that are engaging, informative, and on-brand."

  - q: "What is the ideal blog post length for SEO?"
    a:
      - "1,500–2,500 words for most topics. Long enough to cover the subject thoroughly, short enough to maintain reader interest. Bazam creates blog posts at any length, but for SEO purposes, the agent naturally produces comprehensive content that satisfies search intent."
