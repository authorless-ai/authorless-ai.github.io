---
title: "Campaign Management & Planning"
description: "How to plan, create, and manage marketing campaigns — from product launches to seasonal promotions."
faq:
  - q: "How do I create a marketing campaign?"
    a:
      - "In Bazam.ai, define your campaign goals, target audience, and themes. The AI agent generates all the content — blog posts, social campaigns, ad copy, and email newsletters — aligned to your campaign objectives. Schedule everything and the campaign runs on autopilot."

  - q: "What is a content marketing campaign?"
    a:
      - "A coordinated set of content pieces designed to achieve a specific goal — product launch, brand awareness, lead generation, or seasonal promotion. Bazam.ai creates campaigns with content for every channel, each piece serving a strategic purpose in the overall plan."

  - q: "How do I plan a product launch campaign?"
    a:
      - "Tell Bazam.ai about the product, launch date, and target audience. The agent creates a complete launch pack — pre-launch teasers, launch day announcements, social campaigns, blog posts, press releases, and follow-up content. Schedule everything from one dashboard."

  - q: "How do I create seasonal marketing campaigns?"
    a:
      - "Tell the agent your seasonal theme and target audience. It generates holiday campaigns, back-to-school promotions, summer sales, or Black Friday content across all platforms. Plan seasonal campaigns months in advance and schedule everything."

  - q: "How do I manage UTM tracking for campaigns?"
    a:
      - "Bazam.ai supports UTM tracking at the campaign level. Define your tracking parameters and they're applied to all content published under that campaign. Track which campaigns drive traffic and conversions without manual URL building."

  - q: "How do I create content for different campaign stages?"
    a:
      - "Define the campaign phases — teaser, launch, sustain, close — and the agent creates content appropriate to each stage. Pre-launch generates anticipation, launch day drives action, sustain maintains interest, and close creates urgency."

  - q: "How do I run multiple campaigns at the same time?"
    a:
      - "Create separate campaigns in Bazam.ai, each with their own goals, audiences, and content. The agent produces content for all campaigns simultaneously. Filter your calendar by campaign to see what's scheduled for each one."

  - q: "What is a content profile?"
    a:
      - "A reusable template in Bazam.ai that defines content type, tone, format, and purpose. Map content profiles to your campaign stages — awareness posts, educational articles, conversion content. They ensure every piece serves a strategic purpose."

  - q: "How do I create a visual style guide for campaigns?"
    a:
      - "Define brand visual guidelines in Bazam.ai — colours, fonts, and image style preferences for each campaign. AI-generated images follow these guidelines to maintain visual consistency across all campaign content."

  - q: "How do I measure campaign success?"
    a:
      - "Bazam.ai tracks engagement, reach, clicks, and growth for all campaign content. Ask the agent for a campaign performance summary. Compare metrics across campaigns to identify what works and replicate successful approaches."
