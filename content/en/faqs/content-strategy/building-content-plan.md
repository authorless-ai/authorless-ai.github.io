faq:
  - q: "How do I create a content marketing plan?"
    a:
      - "Define your audience, choose your channels, set a publishing frequency, and plan your topic mix. Bazam simplifies each step — define audience personas in the platform, connect your publishing channels, use AI content suggestions for topics mapped to the buyer journey, and visualise everything on the content calendar."

  - q: "What should a content marketing plan include?"
    a:
      - "Target audience, content themes, publishing frequency, channel strategy, and measurement approach. In Bazam, these map directly to platform features — audience personas, content suggestions, the content calendar, connected publishing channels, and the analytics dashboard."

  - q: "How do I set realistic content marketing goals?"
    a:
      - "Start with what you can sustain. One blog post per week and 3–5 social posts per week is a strong starting point. Bazam's AI wizards make this achievable — a month's content in one session. Set goals around consistency first, then increase volume as you build your workflow."

  - q: "How do I decide what to write about?"
    a:
      - "Ask what your audience needs to know at each stage of their buying decision. Use Bazam's content suggestions — the AI generates topic ideas from your audience personas, business profile, and existing content gaps. Every suggestion tells you what to create and why it matters strategically."

  - q: "How do I balance different types of content?"
    a:
      - "Follow the 80/20 rule: 80% value-driven educational content, 20% direct promotional content. Bazam's strategy framework maps content to AIDA stages  — ensuring your mix includes attention-grabbing, trust-building, and conversion-driving pieces in the right proportions."

  - q: "How do I plan content for a new business with no audience?"
    a:
      - "Start with awareness content. Define who your ideal customer is and create content answering their questions before they know about you. Bazam's audience personas and content suggestions help you target content from day one — even without historical data."

  - q: "How often should I review my content strategy?"
    a:
      - "Monthly for tactical adjustments, quarterly for strategic review. Check Bazam's analytics monthly to see which content types, topics, and platforms are performing. Quarterly, review your overall strategy — are you reaching the right audience? Is your funnel balanced?"

  - q: "What is the difference between content strategy and content marketing?"
    a:
      - "Strategy is the plan — who you're targeting, why, and what success looks like. Marketing is the execution — creating, publishing, and measuring content. Bazam supports both — audience personas and suggestions for strategy; wizards, calendar, and analytics for execution."

  - q: "How do I create a content plan when I have no marketing experience?"
    a:
      - "Use a platform with built-in strategy. Bazam's content suggestions are mapped to the AIDA buyer journey — the platform tells you what to create and why. The content calendar organises your schedule. You don't need marketing experience; the framework is built in."

  - q: "How do I align content marketing with business goals?"
    a:
      - "Connect content types to outcomes. Awareness content drives traffic. Educational content builds trust. Conversion content drives sales. Bazam labels each piece with its strategic purpose — so every post connects back to a business objective, not just a publishing schedule."
