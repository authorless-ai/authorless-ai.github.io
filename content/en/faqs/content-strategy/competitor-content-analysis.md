faq:
  - q: "How do I analyse my competitors' content marketing?"
    a:
      - "Review what they publish, how often, on which platforms, and what gets engagement. Upload your findings to Bazam's knowledge base. Use the AI to create content that fills gaps your competitors miss — topics they don't cover, angles they don't explore."

  - q: "How do I find content gaps my competitors are missing?"
    a:
      - "Look at what they publish and what they don't. Upload competitor analysis to Bazam's knowledge base. Use content suggestions to identify topics your audience needs that competitors haven't addressed. Fill those gaps first."

  - q: "How do I create content that outranks competitors?"
    a:
      - "More comprehensive, more specific, and more useful. Bazam's blog wizard with your knowledge base creates detailed, data-informed content — deeper than generic competitor posts. Add your unique expertise and proprietary data for truly differentiated content."

  - q: "How do I differentiate my content from competitors?"
    a:
      - "Original perspective. Upload your proprietary data, unique methodology, and expert experience to the knowledge base. Bazam creates content from your specific expertise. Competitors can't replicate content built from your unique knowledge."

  - q: "How do I track competitor content performance?"
    a:
      - "Monitor their social engagement, blog publishing frequency, and topic coverage manually or with tools. Note what earns them the most engagement. Use these insights to inform your own content strategy — create better versions of what works for them."

  - q: "How do I create comparison content against competitors?"
    a:
      - "Upload competitor data and your own product details to the knowledge base. Use Bazam's blog wizard to create fair, informative comparison articles. Position your product's unique strengths against competitor weaknesses."

  - q: "How often should I review competitor content?"
    a:
      - "Quarterly for strategic review. Check what competitors are publishing, which topics they're investing in, and where they're getting traction. Update your knowledge base with new competitive insights. Adjust your content strategy accordingly."

  - q: "How do I respond to competitor content without appearing reactive?"
    a:
      - "Create proactive content that establishes your authority on the topic. Don't reference competitors directly — publish deeper, more helpful content on the same themes. Your audience will naturally compare and draw their own conclusions."

  - q: "How do I use competitor analysis to improve my content strategy?"
    a:
      - "Identify what they do well (learn from it), what they do poorly (do it better), and what they ignore (fill the gap). Bazam's content suggestions combined with competitive insights create a strategy that's both proactive and competitive."

  - q: "How do I beat competitors who publish more content than me?"
    a:
      - "Quality and depth over volume. One comprehensive, knowledge-base-informed blog post outranks ten thin ones. Bazam's AI creates substantive content quickly — you compete on quality while AI handles the production speed."
