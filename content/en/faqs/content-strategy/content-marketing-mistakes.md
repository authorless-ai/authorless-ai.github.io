title: "Content Marketing Mistakes to Avoid"
description: "Common content marketing mistakes and how to avoid them — from inconsistency to ignoring analytics and poor targeting."
faq:
  - q: "What are the biggest content marketing mistakes?"
    a:
      - "Inconsistency, no strategy, ignoring analytics, and writing for everyone instead of a specific audience. Bazam prevents the first three — the content calendar enforces consistency, content suggestions provide strategy, and analytics track results. Audience personas solve the fourth."

  - q: "Why is my content marketing not working?"
    a:
      - "Usually: inconsistent publishing, wrong topics for your audience, or poor distribution. Check Bazam's analytics — are you publishing regularly? Is engagement declining? The data reveals the problem. Content suggestions aligned to audience personas fix the targeting."

  - q: "How do I fix inconsistent content publishing?"
    a:
      - "Batch-create and schedule in advance. Monthly batching with Bazam's wizards produces your entire month's content in one session. Schedule on the calendar and autopublishing handles distribution. Inconsistency disappears when the system publishes for you."

  - q: "Why is nobody reading my blog?"
    a:
      - "Three likely causes: wrong topics, poor SEO structure, or no promotion. Use Bazam's content suggestions for audience-targeted topics. The blog wizard creates SEO-structured posts. Generate social promotion to drive initial traffic. Fix all three and readership grows."

  - q: "How do I stop publishing content that nobody engages with?"
    a:
      - "Stop guessing and start using data. Review Bazam's analytics — which content gets engagement, which doesn't. Create more of what works. Use content suggestions based on audience personas instead of random topic selection."

  - q: "Why is my social media content not getting engagement?"
    a:
      - "Generic content, wrong platforms, or inconsistent posting. Analytics reveal which is the problem. Bazam shows per-platform engagement — maybe you're investing in the wrong channel. Content suggestions ensure topics match audience interests."

  - q: "How do I avoid creating duplicate content?"
    a:
      - "Use a shared content calendar. Everyone on the team sees the same schedule — which topics are covered, which dates are filled. Bazam's suggestions also check against your publishing history to avoid recommending topics you've recently covered."

  - q: "What happens if I publish content without a strategy?"
    a:
      - "Wasted effort. Random content rarely builds momentum — it doesn't compound, doesn't build authority, and doesn't move buyers through a funnel. Bazam's AIDA framework and content suggestions provide built-in strategy so every post has a purpose."

  - q: "How do I avoid brand inconsistency in content?"
    a:
      - "Centralise your brand voice. Bazam enforces voice settings on every piece — tone, vocabulary, personality — regardless of who creates it. Brand inconsistency happens when multiple people interpret 'the brand' differently. Centralised settings remove interpretation."

  - q: "How do I recover from a content marketing failure?"
    a:
      - "Reset your strategy. Review analytics to understand what went wrong. Redefine your audience personas. Use Bazam's content suggestions for fresh, strategically aligned topics. Start publishing consistently and track improvement. Recovery is about systems, not heroics."
