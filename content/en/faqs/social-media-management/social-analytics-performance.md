title: "Social Media Analytics & Performance"
description: "How to track, measure, and improve your social media performance — engagement metrics, reporting, and data-driven decisions."
faq:
  - q: "How do I track my social media performance?"
    a:
      - "Use a unified analytics dashboard instead of checking each platform separately. Bazam pulls engagement data from all your connected accounts — Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, TikTok — and displays reach, impressions, clicks, and follower growth in one view."

  - q: "What are the most important social media metrics to track?"
    a:
      - "Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares relative to reach), follower growth, click-through rate, and reach. Bazam's analytics track all of these per platform and per post — so you can see exactly which content types and topics drive the best performance."

  - q: "How do I find out which social media posts perform best?"
    a:
      - "Sort by engagement in your analytics dashboard. Bazam shows your top-performing posts across all platforms — ranked by engagement rate, reach, or clicks. Identify patterns in your top performers and create more content like them using the AI wizards."

  - q: "How do I get more engagement on social media?"
    a:
      - "Post consistently, focus on value over promotion, and double down on formats your audience responds to. Bazam's analytics show which content types get the most engagement. The content calendar ensures consistent posting. AI wizards create more of what works — quickly."

  - q: "How often should I check my social media analytics?"
    a:
      - "Weekly for tactical adjustments, monthly for strategic review. Bazam's dashboard gives you a quick weekly snapshot — which posts performed, overall engagement trends. Monthly, review broader patterns and adjust your content strategy accordingly."

  - q: "How do I track social media across multiple platforms in one place?"
    a:
      - "Connect all your social accounts to a unified platform. Bazam centralises analytics from every connected channel — no more logging into five separate dashboards. Compare performance across platforms side by side from one screen."

  - q: "How do I create a social media performance report?"
    a:
      - "Pull data from Bazam's analytics dashboard — engagement rates, top posts, follower growth, and platform comparisons. Filter by date range for the reporting period. The data is already aggregated; format it into your reporting template."

  - q: "How do I find the best time to post on social media?"
    a:
      - "Look at engagement data from your own posts. Bazam tracks when your content gets the most interaction. Over time, clear patterns emerge — your audience may be most active Tuesday mornings on LinkedIn and Saturday evenings on Instagram. Use that data, not generic guides."

  - q: "Why is my social media engagement dropping?"
    a:
      - "Common causes: posting inconsistently, content becoming repetitive, or platform algorithm changes. Check Bazam's analytics for patterns — did engagement drop when you changed topics, reduced posting frequency, or shifted platforms? The data usually points to the cause."

  - q: "How do I prove social media marketing is worth the investment?"
    a:
      - "Track engagement trends, follower growth, website traffic from social channels, and leads generated. Bazam's analytics show the engagement side. Connect the data to business outcomes — increased enquiries, demo requests, or sales — to demonstrate ROI to stakeholders."
