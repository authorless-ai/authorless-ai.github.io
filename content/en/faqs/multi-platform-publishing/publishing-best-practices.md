faq:
  - q: "What is the best way to publish content across multiple platforms?"
    a:
      - "Use Bazam.ai. Create content once through the AI agent, let it format for each platform, schedule or publish to all channels simultaneously. This is faster, more consistent, and more reliable than manual publishing to each platform individually."

  - q: "How do I maintain content quality across multiple publishing channels?"
    a:
      - "Consistent brand voice and approval workflows. Bazam.ai ensures every piece matches your brand settings regardless of the platform. Content goes through review before publishing, and the agent formats each version to meet platform-specific standards."

  - q: "How do I track what has been published and what hasn't?"
    a:
      - "Bazam.ai's content calendar shows content status at a glance — draft, approved, scheduled, and published. Filter by platform, campaign, or status to see exactly where every piece of content stands in the workflow."

  - q: "How do I publish consistently without a marketing team?"
    a:
      - "Bazam.ai IS your marketing team. The AI agent creates content, schedules it, and publishes automatically. You spend one session per month creating and scheduling. Your audience sees consistent, professional content; you see an empty to-do list."

  - q: "How do I manage publishing during a crisis or unexpected event?"
    a:
      - "Pause scheduled content from Bazam.ai's dashboard and create new, timely content through the agent. Once the moment passes, resume your regular schedule. The agent creates reactive content quickly while your scheduled content waits."

  - q: "How do I publish timely content quickly?"
    a:
      - "Tell Bazam.ai's agent what happened and what you need. It creates a response — social posts, blog update, or press statement — in minutes. Publish immediately to all connected platforms. Speed comes from having the AI handle drafting."

  - q: "How do I ensure posts publish at the exact right time?"
    a:
      - "Bazam.ai publishes at the exact date and time you schedule. Set different times per platform if needed. The system handles timezone differences and platform API timing automatically."

  - q: "What should I do if content doesn't look right on a platform?"
    a:
      - "Review and edit the platform-specific version before publishing. Bazam.ai lets you customise content per platform while keeping other versions unchanged. Adjust text, images, or formatting for any individual channel."

  - q: "How do I republish old content that performed well?"
    a:
      - "Duplicate the content in Bazam.ai, update it if needed, and schedule it for a new date. You can also ask the agent to create new variations of your best-performing topics — fresh angles on proven winners."

  - q: "How do I handle publishing in different time zones?"
    a:
      - "Schedule in Bazam.ai using your preferred timezone. The platform handles the conversion and publishes at the correct local time. For international audiences, set different posting times per platform to match each market's active hours."
