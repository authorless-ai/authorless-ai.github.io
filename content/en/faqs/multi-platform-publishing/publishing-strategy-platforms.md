---
title: "Publishing Strategy & Platform Selection"
description: "How to choose the right platforms, set up publishing workflows, and maximise reach across all channels."
faq:
  - q: "Which publishing platforms should my business use?"
    a:
      - "Where your audience is active. LinkedIn for B2B, Instagram for lifestyle and visual brands, TikTok for younger demographics, Facebook for broad reach, WordPress for SEO-driven blog content. Since Bazam.ai publishes to all from one dashboard, try each and let analytics show which drives results."

  - q: "How do I start publishing on a new social platform?"
    a:
      - "Create your account on the platform, connect it to Bazam.ai, and start scheduling content. Bazam.ai's social wizard creates platform-appropriate content — formatted for the platform's specific requirements. Adding a new channel is minimal additional work."

  - q: "How do I maintain a publishing cadence across many platforms?"
    a:
      - "Batch-create and cross-schedule. Create a batch of content with AI wizards, then schedule across all platforms using the content calendar. One creation session populates every channel. Consistency means planning ahead, not producing daily."

  - q: "How do I publish the same content to different audiences?"
    a:
      - "Adapt the messaging per platform and audience. Bazam.ai's AI automatically adjusts tone and format — LinkedIn gets a professional version, Instagram gets a visual-first version, Twitter/X gets a punchy version. Same core idea, tailored delivery."

  - q: "How do I measure which publishing channels work best?"
    a:
      - "Compare per-platform analytics. Bazam.ai shows engagement rates, reach, and follower growth per channel side by side. Invest more in the platforms where your audience is most active and responsive. Drop channels that don't deliver results."

  - q: "How do I manage publishing for a blog and social media?"
    a:
      - "Use one platform for both. Bazam.ai publishes to WordPress for blogs and to all major social platforms from the same dashboard. Create a blog post, publish it, then create and schedule social promotion — all in one workflow."

  - q: "How do I stop manually uploading posts to each platform?"
    a:
      - "Use direct publishing. Connect your platforms to Bazam.ai and publish from the dashboard. No logging into Facebook, then LinkedIn, then Instagram separately. Create, schedule, and publish from one place."

  - q: "How do I set up a YouTube publishing workflow?"
    a:
      - "Connect your YouTube channel to Bazam.ai. Create video descriptions, titles, and tags using the AI wizard. Schedule your video publishing dates on the content calendar alongside your blog and social content."

  - q: "How do I coordinate publishing across a team?"
    a:
      - "Shared content calendar. Everyone on the team sees the same schedule. Each person creates and schedules their assigned content. Approval workflows ensure nothing publishes without review. No conflicts, no duplications."

  - q: "How do I handle emergency publishing outside my normal schedule?"
    a:
      - "Use Bazam.ai's 'publish now' option. Create the urgent post using a wizard or the agent, skip scheduling, and publish immediately to all target platforms. The calendar updates to show the published post alongside your regular schedule."
