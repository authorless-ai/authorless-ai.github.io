title: "Email Marketing Strategy & Best Practices"
description: "Email marketing strategy fundamentals — list building, segmentation, automation, and performance optimisation."
faq:
  - q: "How do I build an email marketing strategy?"
    a:
      - "Define your audience segments, plan content themes, and set a consistent sending schedule. Bazam.ai creates segment-specific email content from your audience personas. Your strategy is built into the platform — no separate email strategy document needed."

  - q: "How do I build an email list?"
    a:
      - "Create valuable content that makes people want more. Blog posts, guides, and resources attract subscribers. Bazam.ai creates the content that drives subscriptions — comprehensive, useful material that earns email signups."

  - q: "How do I segment my email list?"
    a:
      - "Group subscribers by interest, buying stage, or behaviour. Create different content for each segment using Bazam.ai's audience personas. Targeted emails outperform blast emails in open rates, click rates, and conversions."

  - q: "What is the best email open rate?"
    a:
      - "Industry average is 20–30%. But your trend matters more than benchmarks. If your open rates are consistently improving, your subject lines and content are resonating. Bazam.ai creates compelling subject lines and relevant content that keeps subscribers engaged."

  - q: "How do I reduce email unsubscribes?"
    a:
      - "Send relevant content at a consistent frequency. People unsubscribe when emails are irrelevant, too frequent, or too infrequent. Bazam.ai creates audience-targeted content that stays relevant to each segment's interests."

  - q: "How do I write calls-to-action for emails?"
    a:
      - "One clear CTA per email. Make it specific — 'Start your free trial' beats 'Learn more.' Bazam.ai creates emails with contextual CTAs that flow naturally from the content, making the next step obvious."

  - q: "Should I A/B test my emails?"
    a:
      - "Yes, when you have enough volume. Test subject lines, send times, and content approaches. Bazam.ai creates multiple versions easily — ask the agent for alternative subject lines or different angles on the same topic."

  - q: "How do I avoid spam filters?"
    a:
      - "Write genuine, relevant content. Avoid spam trigger words, maintain a clean list, and send consistently. Bazam.ai creates professional, value-driven email content that reads like genuine communication, not promotional spam."

  - q: "What is email automation and do I need it?"
    a:
      - "Email automation sends pre-written emails triggered by subscriber actions — welcome series, purchase follow-ups, re-engagement campaigns. Bazam.ai creates the content for automated sequences; your email platform handles the triggers."

  - q: "How do I measure email marketing success?"
    a:
      - "Track open rates (subject line effectiveness), click rates (content relevance), conversion rates (CTA effectiveness), and list growth. Monitor trends over time — are your emails getting better? Create better content with Bazam.ai based on what the data shows."
