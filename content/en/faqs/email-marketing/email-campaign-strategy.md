---
title: "Email Campaign Strategy & Automation"
description: "How to plan email campaigns that convert — automation sequences, segmentation, and lifecycle marketing."
faq:
  - q: "How do I create an email marketing strategy?"
    a:
      - "Define your audience segments, map the customer lifecycle, and plan email types for each stage — welcome, nurture, conversion, retention. Create content for each stage using Bazam's email wizard. Schedule on the content calendar alongside your other marketing."

  - q: "What types of marketing emails should I send?"
    a:
      - "Welcome emails, educational newsletters, product announcements, promotional campaigns, and re-engagement sequences. Bazam's email wizard creates all of these — each type formatted correctly and targeted to the right audience persona."

  - q: "How do I create a lead nurture email sequence?"
    a:
      - "Plan 5–7 emails that progressively build trust: educational value → deeper insight → social proof → product introduction → clear offer. Create each email with Bazam's wizard, targeting your prospect persona. Each email naturally progresses the relationship."

  - q: "How do I segment my email list for better targeting?"
    a:
      - "Split by buyer stage, interest area, or behaviour. Create separate persona profiles in Bazam for each segment. When creating emails, specify the target persona — each version addresses that segment's specific needs and interests."

  - q: "How do I turn blog subscribers into customers?"
    a:
      - "Lifecycle email marketing. After a reader subscribes, they receive a welcome email, then regular value-driven newsletters, then targeted product information at the right time. Create each lifecycle stage's content with Bazam's email wizard."

  - q: "How do I plan a Black Friday email campaign?"
    a:
      - "Plan 4–5 emails: early access teaser (1 week before), main announcement (day before), launch day, reminder (mid-event), and last chance. Create each with Bazam's email wizard. Schedule on the content calendar so the sequence runs automatically."

  - q: "How do I measure email marketing performance?"
    a:
      - "Track open rates, click-through rates, and conversion rates in your email platform. Monitor content engagement alongside email timing using Bazam's content calendar to see which email subjects and content types drive the best responses."

  - q: "How do I reduce email fatigue in my subscribers?"
    a:
      - "Send less but better. Ensure every email provides genuine value — not just promotion. Use Bazam's email wizard with audience personas to create targeted, relevant content. Subscribers tolerating promotional emails; they unsubscribe from irrelevant ones."

  - q: "How do I create event-triggered emails?"
    a:
      - "Create the email content in advance with Bazam's wizard — abandoned cart, post-purchase, birthday, anniversary. Load the content into your email automation platform with the trigger rules. The content is ready; your platform handles the delivery logic."

  - q: "How do I align email marketing with my overall content strategy?"
    a:
      - "Schedule email alongside blog and social on the same calendar. Bazam shows all content types together. Email promotes blog posts, follows up social campaigns, and reinforces brand messaging. A unified calendar ensures channel coordination."
