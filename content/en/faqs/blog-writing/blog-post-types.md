  - q: "What types of blog posts should I write?"
    a:
      - "A mix: how-to guides (step-by-step solutions), listicles (curated lists), opinion pieces (your unique perspective), case studies (proof of results), and FAQ compilations (answering common questions). Bazam.ai creates all types from your knowledge base and audience data."

  - q: "What is a how-to blog post?"
    a:
      - "A step-by-step guide that teaches your reader how to do something specific. How-to posts are SEO powerhouses because they match high-intent searches. Bazam.ai creates comprehensive how-to posts from your expertise and knowledge base."

  - q: "What is a listicle and should I write them?"
    a:
      - "A listicle is a list-format article — '10 ways to improve X', '7 tools for Y.' Yes, write them. They're scannable, shareable, and rank well. Bazam.ai creates well-researched listicles from your knowledge base that go beyond generic advice."

  - q: "How do I write case study blog posts?"
    a:
      - "Upload case study data to Bazam.ai's knowledge base. The agent creates compelling narratives from your results — challenges, solutions, and outcomes. Real numbers and results make case study posts your most convincing content."

  - q: "How do I write opinion pieces for my blog?"
    a:
      - "Share your genuine perspective on industry topics. Bazam.ai creates opinion pieces in your authentic voice — not generic takes that anyone could write. Define your contrarian or unique viewpoints and the agent builds them into substantive articles."

  - q: "What is a comparison blog post?"
    a:
      - "A post comparing options — products, tools, approaches. 'X vs Y: which is better for Z.' These posts capture high-intent commercial searches. Bazam.ai creates informed comparison posts using your knowledge base and competitive research."

  - q: "How do I write a blog post that generates leads?"
    a:
      - "Address a prospect's specific problem with actionable advice, then offer your solution naturally. Bazam.ai creates buyer-journey-aligned content — educational posts that build trust and strategic CTAs that invite next steps."

  - q: "What is a pillar blog post?"
    a:
      - "A comprehensive guide covering a broad topic in depth — 3,000+ words covering all aspects with links to related cluster posts. Bazam.ai creates pillar content that establishes topical authority and serves as the hub for your content cluster."

  - q: "How do I write a roundup or curated blog post?"
    a:
      - "Curate insights from your industry — trends, tools, tips — and add your commentary. Bazam.ai's agent compiles and structures roundup content from your knowledge base and industry context."

  - q: "Which blog post format drives the most traffic?"
    a:
      - "How-to guides and comprehensive FAQ pages typically drive the most organic traffic because they match high-intent informational searches. Bazam.ai creates both — and can combine them into pillar pages that dominate search results."
