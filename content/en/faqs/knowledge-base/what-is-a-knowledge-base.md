  - q: "Why do I need a knowledge base for AI content?"
    a:
      - "Without a knowledge base, AI generates from general training data — technically correct but lacking specifics. With a knowledge base, AI content platforms like Bazam.ai reference your actual products, pricing, case studies, and positioning. The result is content that reads like it was written by someone who knows your business."

  - q: "What happens if I don't use a knowledge base with AI?"
    a:
      - "You get generic content. The AI has no information about your products, pricing, or competitive advantages — so it writes vague marketing copy that could describe any company. A knowledge base in AI platforms like Bazam.ai is the difference between generic filler and specific, informed content."

  - q: "How does a knowledge base improve AI content quality?"
    a:
