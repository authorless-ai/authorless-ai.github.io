title: "Media Library & Visual Content Management"
description: "How to manage images, videos, and visual assets for your marketing content — storage, organisation, and reuse."
faq:
  - q: "How do I organise marketing images and assets?"
    a:
      - "Use a media library. Bazam.ai stores all your uploaded images, graphics, and visual assets in a centralised library. Upload once, reuse across blog posts, social media, and email. No more hunting through folders or duplicating files."

  - q: "Where should I store images for my blog and social content?"
    a:
      - "In your content platform's media library. Bazam.ai's library keeps all visual assets alongside your content — attach images to posts, reuse them across campaigns, and organise by project. Everything is in one place."

  - q: "How do I add images to blog posts and social media?"
    a:
      - "Upload to Bazam.ai's media library, then attach images to any post — featured images for blogs, social post images, email headers. When content publishes, images publish with it automatically."

  - q: "How do I reuse images across multiple pieces of content?"
    a:
      - "Access your media library when creating any content. Previously uploaded images are available for reuse — no re-uploading. Feature the same brand photos across blog posts, social updates, and email campaigns."

  - q: "How do I manage visual assets for multiple clients?"
    a:
      - "Each client's project has its own media library. Client A's brand images are stored in Client A's project. No risk of using Client B's visuals in the wrong campaign. Complete isolation per project."

  - q: "How do I create consistent visual branding across all content?"
    a:
      - "Store your brand assets — logos, brand colours, approved photos, templates — in the media library. When creating content, draw from these approved assets. Consistency comes from using the same visual library for every post across every channel."

  - q: "What image formats are supported in AI content tools?"
    a:
      - "Standard web formats — JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP. Bazam.ai's media library accepts the formats your publishing platforms require. Upload high-quality originals and the platform handles optimisation for each channel."

  - q: "How do I manage featured images for blog posts?"
    a:
      - "Upload featured images to the media library and attach them to blog posts before publishing. When Bazam.ai publishes to WordPress, the featured image is included automatically — properly formatted and positioned."

  - q: "How do I keep my media library organised as it grows?"
    a:
      - "Organise by project — each client or brand maintains its own library. Within projects, images are associated with the content they're used in. Regular housekeeping — removing unused assets — keeps the library manageable."

  - q: "Can I generate images with AI for my marketing content?"
    a:
      - "AI image generation is evolving rapidly. Use dedicated image generation tools to create visuals, then upload them to Bazam.ai's media library and attach them to your content. The best workflow combines AI text creation with AI-generated or curated visuals."
